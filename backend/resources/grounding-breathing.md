# Grounding and breathing techniques

## Box breathing
Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold empty for 4, then repeat. The equal, steady rhythm is easy to remember under stress and helps regulate the nervous system quickly.

## Physical grounding
Pressing your feet firmly into the floor, or holding something with a distinct texture in your hands, gives your nervous system a clear physical anchor when your thoughts feel like they're spinning too fast to catch.

## Orienting to the room
Slowly turn your head and name a few ordinary objects around you out loud — a chair, a window, a light switch. This "orienting" reflex reminds your nervous system that you are safe, here, and now, rather than caught in whatever your mind is replaying.

## Progressive muscle relaxation
Starting at your feet and working upward, tense each muscle group for about 5 seconds, then release. Working through the whole body this way, one section at a time, helps release tension that's built up without you fully noticing it.
